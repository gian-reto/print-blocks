# Monthly site energy template

Use this template for home energy summaries, commercial site energy reports, solar installation check-ins, or monthly facility handoff notes.

It contains:

- a centered monthly energy header
- site and period metadata rows
- a stacked area chart for weekly energy mix
- an area chart for daily site load across the month
- summary rows for solar, grid, battery, and self-use
- a short follow-up note

Customize the site name, period, weekly energy mix values, daily load values, totals, and follow-up recommendation. Keep chart labels short when possible so the report remains readable on receipt-width paper.

This template is only a starting point. You may ignore it and submit any custom print request that matches the print schema.
